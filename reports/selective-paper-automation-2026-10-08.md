# Individual-stock paper automation

**TRADING BEHAVIOUR CHANGED.** The production paper orchestrator now installs `SelectivePaperSleeve` in the `quality_momentum` slot alongside `index_directional`. It does not install the old Quality 50 strategy. This is the separately identified `selective-paper-v1` forward paper trial; no claim of profitable returns or live eligibility is made.

The missing path was executable stock candidates: the engine previously stripped all stock proposals before allocation. It now reads the same dated selective evidence used by Ideas, filters to verified liquid NSE constituents, and automatically publishes zero to three qualifying plans with one per sector. A UI visit is unnecessary. Existing quality, turnover, relative-strength, volume/delivery, entry-zone, stop and target predicates are unchanged. The new sleeve receives first claim on allocation; its existing exposure cap and position count remain unchanged.

Entry requires an observed post-publication pullback into the original zone, a completed daily rebound with the existing volume/range predicates, and a strictly later quote on the next NSE session. Forming daily candles are excluded. OFF/unknown regimes, missing membership, stale or adverse evidence and failed book risk do not authorize entries. Observations run in the fast paper service even with no pending orders. Every confirmation predicate is archived with its contemporaneous evidence.

The central exchange reserves the approved quantity and creates a durable pending order. A fill requires a later identified bid/ask snapshot, sourced instrument/session rules, sufficient unconsumed displayed depth and another account-risk check. The original entry zone cannot widen; stop/target rounding follows the sourced tick. A publication-derived idempotency key survives cycles and restarts. An old signal or forged publication cannot authorize a stock order. Evidence/news, epoch, feature flag and original model/levels are checked again before fills. Reduced account quantities must still clear the frozen target-cost assumptions.

Actual house fills retain model/publication identity in their order receipt and position rationale. Subscriber orders require that owned exchange fill and retain its zone/model/provenance, with independent account sizing and later fills. Stops, targets and time exits use the existing owned position lifecycle; subscriber trade rows retain the model version and ledger reconciliation remains exact. `quality_momentum` is absent from the broker mirror allowlist; this trial cannot automatically place real-money entries.

The paper capital remains ₹10,000; stock exposure is capped at 30% with one stock position, index exposure at 50% with one index position, and the overall position limit remains three. Existing 2% per-trade ceiling, 1.5% daily loss limit, 10% drawdown brake and ₹1,500 minimum ticket are unchanged. Sizing includes costs and slippage. Small capital, strict evidence or regime gates can legitimately produce no qualifying trade; there is no activity quota.

The dedicated `equity_automation.db` journal is separate from user research publications, books and the registered v2 experiment. Existing research fingerprints and histories are unchanged; trial fingerprints additionally bind the paper epoch/model. `app.screening.automation.report(epoch)` reads compact observation counts, not hypothetical touches represented as fills. Current paper orders, positions and trades remain the sources of actual execution outcomes.

The home page now describes individual-stock paper automation and its confirmation conditions without claiming validated returns. Index-desk text no longer claims that only an ETF can trade. Scope responses expose the actual stock model and validation status; disabled/missing capability states remain explicit.

Verification: the real production `sleeve_pass` runs against disposable sourced fixtures and queues a confirmed individual stock, with no submission-time fill; the later exchange service fills it. Tests also cover automatic publication without UI visits, house/subscriber protection and exit, costed cash-ledger reconciliation, duplicate/restart identity, forming candles, adverse news, unsupported/forged plans, stale confirmation, epoch identity, unavailable evidence and broker exclusion. Synthetic favourable exits establish lifecycle correctness only. The registered research database is byte-identical after trial processing.

Local verification: 2,605 unittest cases run, 2,472 passed and 133 skipped, zero failures/errors; 103 research/UI function fixtures passed. External sockets were blocked, books were disposable and dotenv loading disabled. No production deployment, book reset, broker-setting change, real order or claim of independent strategy validation is included. Deployment/model promotion still requires the separate concrete authorization specified in the release plan.
