## Subscriber mirror correction

**TRADING BEHAVIOUR CHANGED:** Previously, the house might approve one share
after its risk calculation while each subscriber book independently bought
multiple shares by its own cash-slot rule. Mirrored user-paper entries now take
the smaller of the user's affordable quantity and the house-approved share
count. Manual user buys remain independently sized. This prevents a mirror
from amplifying a house risk decision; it does not turn the stock screen into
a profitable strategy.
