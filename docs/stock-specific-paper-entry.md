# Stock-specific paper entry

**TRADING BEHAVIOUR CHANGED.** `selective-paper-v3` removes the blanket
Nifty-trend veto for individual paper stocks. The completed trend is retained
as measured context and in performance attribution; OFF is displayed as
“Defensive market”, not as a system switch. Unknown market evidence still
blocks entry. Data freshness, dated quality/participation, original entry zone,
observed touch, completed rebound, later executable ask, costs, independent
account risk, owned stops/targets and exit management remain required.

The shared `stock-specific-paper-v1` policy covers house reservation, owned
paper approval, subscriber delivery, fill and read-only readiness. Live
contracts retain their supportive-regime gate; no broker route is promoted.
Index levels are context and cannot be bought as cash shares. This change
does not certify derivative routing or profitability.

Ideas exposes two distinct collections:

- **Entry plans**: the existing strict conditional plan selection. Publication
  is not confirmation or a fill.
- **Research**: up to three quality/relative-strength candidates, one per
  sector, waiting for price structure or participation. Conditional levels
  and quantities use the same ₹10,000 account allocator and after-cost checks.
  Missing/adverse evidence and account risk still exclude candidates. These
  rows show their entry blockers and allow watchlisting/chart review only;
  they cannot authorise an order or enter the forward fill/outcome journal.

No thresholds, portfolio epoch or registered experiment were reset. The
original research protocol keeps its original market gate. Old automation
publications remain archived, with a distinct model fingerprint; new policy
does not reinterpret old observations as confirmed entries.

The stock-by-stock quality/momentum design is consistent with the distinction
between constituent selection and index performance described by
[NSE Indices](https://niftyindices.com/indices/equity/strategy-indices/nifty-total-market-momentum-quality-50).
That source does not validate this application's entry policy or returns.

Acceptance: synthetic production-pass → reservation → later house fill →
owned subscriber fill → stop exit reconciles in defensive context, preserving
the actual regime tag. Unknown context, old model bindings, adverse evidence,
stale quotes and insufficient risk remain refusals. The UI renders research
levels/reasons and cannot submit them. Tests are lifecycle evidence, not alpha.
