# Paper workspace and execution repair

**TRADING BEHAVIOUR CHANGED.** Stock confirmations are refreshed from current
source evidence between expensive screen cycles, and a house reservation or
fill no longer invalidates the same signal for an independently risk-checked
subscriber. Quote provenance survives the actual production loader. Managed
personal fills round cash legs to paise consistently with the immutable ledger.
No selection threshold, regime rule, capital, portfolio epoch, broker setting or
registered experiment is changed.

Home shows account balances, screening counts, pending buys, completed trades,
worker status, source availability and owned protection. Ideas separates qualified
entry plans from a dated research watch with visible failed predicates. Portfolio
defaults to all owned positions with sleeve, regime, stop, target and quote time.
Orders separates lifecycle requests from fills and reports failed loads explicitly.
Personal allocation, order history and attribution use the caller's current epoch;
historical trades remain stored. Allocation no longer silently reads house holdings.

The research watch is **not** a fallback buy list. It neither publishes weaker
ideas nor approves, sizes or funds orders. Duplicate identities and nonfinite
scores cannot be ranked. Source counts do not certify an instrument or model.

`scripts/refresh_exchange_session.py` and the accompanying service/timer capture
official Upstox dated NSE timings and current exchange phase every minute. A
NORMAL_OPEN phase must agree with the exact day's hours. Current observations
expire after three minutes; failures cannot borrow an earlier open observation.
Raw responses are privately hashed and archived without request credentials.
An after-hours closed or unavailable session is not a stock-quote outage.

Verification includes the real loader, automatic publication/confirmation,
pending refresh after 121 seconds, house/subscriber independence, duplicate
delivery, current-epoch isolation, owned allocation, target exit, automatically
triggered stop exit and exact costed cash reconciliation. Browser fixtures are
disposable, synthetic and explicitly labelled; they are not performance evidence.

Remaining release dependencies: reliable **current instrument rules** and
restriction/corporate-action coverage still need an authenticated source-backed
producer and production verification. A session ingester alone does not supply
those rules. Unknown rules continue to refuse orders. OFF still blocks new longs.
Independent after-cost stock-model profitability and a relevant forward paper
record remain unproven. This repair must not be described as a profitable or
fully trading-ready commercial release.
